# 📊 Sample Dataset Information

## comprehensive-sample-expenses.csv

A comprehensive expense dataset with **90 transactions** spanning January to April 2026.

### Dataset Statistics

- **Total Transactions**: 90 entries
- **Date Range**: January 5, 2026 - April 28, 2026 (4 months)
- **Categories Covered**: All 28 categories
- **Payment Methods**: Cash, Card, UPI, Net Banking, Cheque
- **Total Amount**: ~₹400,000+ (varies by data)

### Category Distribution

**Food & Dining** (10+ entries)
- grocery: Weekly/monthly shopping
- dine_out: Restaurants, celebrations
- coffee_snacks: Daily cafe visits

**Transportation** (8+ entries)
- fuel: Monthly diesel/petrol/CNG
- public_transport: Bus passes, auto rides
- vehicle_maintenance: Car/bike servicing

**Bills & Utilities** (15+ entries)
- bills_power: Monthly electricity
- bills_wifi: Internet subscription
- bills_mobile: Recharge and data
- bills_water: Quarterly water bill
- rent_mortgage: Monthly rent

**Lifestyle** (12+ entries)
- shopping_clothes: Seasonal shopping
- shopping_electronics: Gadgets, TV, laptop
- entertainment: Movies, concerts, gaming
- subscriptions: Streaming services
- personal_care: Salon, spa, grooming
- fitness_gym: Gym, yoga, trainer

**Medical** (8+ entries)
- medical_consultation: Doctor visits, specialists
- medical_medicines: Prescriptions, vitamins
- medical_tests: Diagnostics
- medical_hospitalization: Emergency treatment
- medical_insurance: Annual renewal

**Gifting & Donations** (6+ entries)
- gifting_personal: Birthdays, anniversaries
- gifting_festivals: Diwali, Valentine's
- donations_charity: Temple, NGO contributions

**Agriculture** (15+ entries) - Comprehensive farming expenses
- agri_seeds_fertilizers: Seasonal purchase
- agri_equipment: Pump, tractor attachments
- agri_irrigation: Drip system maintenance
- agri_livestock: Dairy cows purchase
- agri_labor: Harvesting payments
- agri_land_lease: Land lease payments
- agri_crop_insurance: Insurance premium
- agri_loan_interest: EMI payments
- agri_storage_transport: Warehouse charges
- agri_other: Tools and supplies

**Education** (2 entries)
- Online courses
- School fees

**Travel** (3 entries)
- Train tickets
- Hotel bookings
- Weekend trips

**Financial & Others** (10+ entries)
- insurance_general: Home, vehicle insurance
- investments_savings: Mutual fund SIP
- pets: Pet food, veterinary
- household_maintenance: Repairs, servicing
- other: Miscellaneous expenses

### Amount Range

- **Minimum**: ₹180 (groceries)
- **Maximum**: ₹25,000 (medical hospitalization)
- **Average**: ~₹4,500 per transaction
- **Typical ranges**:
  - Daily expenses: ₹200-800
  - Monthly bills: ₹1,500-5,500
  - Major purchases: ₹10,000-25,000

### Tags Used

Common tags for filtering:
- **Frequency**: monthly, weekly, daily, quarterly, annual, seasonal
- **Category**: essential, bills, family, personal, work
- **Purpose**: investment, maintenance, health, leisure, emergency
- **Nature**: farming, transport, education, entertainment
- **Special**: urgent, celebration, festival, organic, eco-friendly

### Realistic Patterns

1. **Regular Bills**: Rent, electricity, water paid monthly
2. **Seasonal Expenses**: Farming activities, clothing purchases
3. **Emergency Costs**: Medical hospitalization, urgent repairs
4. **Investments**: SIP, insurance, equipment
5. **Daily Spending**: Groceries, fuel, coffee, transport
6. **Special Occasions**: Birthdays, festivals, vacations

### Payment Method Distribution

- **Card**: Major purchases, online payments (~35%)
- **Cash**: Daily expenses, local markets (~30%)
- **Net Banking**: Bills, insurance, EMIs (~20%)
- **UPI**: Quick payments, small amounts (~10%)
- **Cheque**: Farming equipment, large transactions (~5%)

## How to Use

1. **Import via Bulk Upload**:
   - Click "Import Data" in the dashboard
   - Select `comprehensive-sample-expenses.csv`
   - Preview and confirm

2. **View Analytics**:
   - Charts will populate automatically
   - See spending patterns across 4 months
   - Analyze category-wise breakdown

3. **Test Filters** (when implemented):
   - Filter by date range
   - Filter by category
   - Search by tags
   - Filter by payment method

4. **Export Testing**:
   - Export all data to CSV/Excel/JSON
   - Generate summary report
   - Verify data integrity
